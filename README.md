# Maven Cycles: Multi-Page Business Intelligence Report
## 📊 Project Overview
This repository contains a comprehensive Power BI data analysis project for Maven Cycles, a global bicycle and accessories retailer. The goal of this project was to transform raw transactional data into an interactive, multi-page dashboard that tracks Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), identifies sales trends, and analyzes customer behavior to drive strategic decision-making.

## 🚀 Key Insights & Executive Summary
### 1. Financial Performance
- Total Revenue: $252M with a healthy Profit Margin of 51.26%.

- Profitability: Total profit stands at $125M. However, recent Month-over-Month (MoM) data shows a significant dip in the current month (CM) vs. the last month (LM), with revenue down by 83.2%.

- Trend: Revenue saw a massive structural incline starting in early 2018, peaking in 2019/2020 at approximately $8.7M per month.

### 2. Product & Category Analysis
- Top Performer: Bikes are the primary revenue driver, contributing $149M (approx. 59%) of total revenue.

- Volume Leader: While Bikes lead in value, Accessories dominate transaction volume, specifically items like the "S-Works TT Helmet" and "Hydration Packs."

- Efficiency: Despite being the smallest category by revenue ($14M), Clothing maintains consistent sales volume across the year.

### 3. Customer & Transaction Behavior
- Gender Split: Sales are nearly balanced, though Male customers lead slightly with a 2.6% margin over Female customers.

- Basket Size: The Average Transaction Value (ATV) is $1.54K, though this has seen a downward trend from 2015-2017 before stabilizing.

- Time Patterns: 71.91% of profits are generated during Weekdays ($90M), compared to 28.09% on Weekends.

### 4. Time Intelligence
- Seasonal Peaks: July 2017 saw a record MoM revenue jump of 141.85%.

- Year-over-Year (YoY): The business has achieved a 19.59% YoY Revenue growth, indicating strong long-term scaling despite short-term fluctuations.

### 🛠️ Dashboard Features
- The report is divided into five specialized views:

- Executive Summary: High-level KPIs (Revenue, Profit, Transactions) and gender-based revenue distribution.

- Sales & Revenue Performance: Deep dive into top/bottom performing products and 3-month moving averages.

- Profitability & Cost Analysis: Waterfall charts showing revenue by category and a comparison of Weekday vs. Weekend profitability.

- Transaction & Customer Behavior: Analysis of transaction frequency and category-specific volume.

- Time Intelligence & Trend Analysis: Advanced MoM and YoY calculations with heatmaps to identify seasonal growth patterns.

### 📂 Technical Stack
- Tool: Power BI Desktop

- Data Modeling: Star Schema (Fact and Dimension tables)

- DAX Measures: * YoY Revenue %,3M Moving Average,MoM % Revenue,Total Profit & Profit Margin %
